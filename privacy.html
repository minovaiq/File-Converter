Privacy Policy

Image files and generated downloads

File Converter reads the images you select and creates output files inside your browser. The conversion tool does not send image contents or filenames to our servers. The browser keeps the selected files and results available while the page is open. Closing or reloading the page clears the conversion list. Files you download remain on your device until you delete them.

Website requests

When you load the website, the hosting infrastructure may process your IP address, browser information, requested page and request time to deliver pages, diagnose problems and protect the site. The converter does not require an account.

Advertising and cookies

If Google AdSense is enabled, Google and other advertising providers may place or read cookies, or use web beacons, IP addresses and other identifiers to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and, where permitted, personalize ads. These identifiers are separate from the image files you select. See how Google uses data on partner sites and Google My Ad Center.

When a privacy choices panel is available, use its controls to review or change your advertising preferences. You can also manage cookies through your browser settings.

Questions about this policy

You can reach the project through the support information. Please keep personal information and private images out of public reports.
